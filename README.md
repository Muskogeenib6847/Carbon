Carbon is a **clipboard manager** for Windows that lives quietly in your system tray, ready to help you every time you copy something. It remembers everything you copy — text, images, files — and keeps it organized and searchable so you never lose important information again.

Imagine copying a password, a link, or a snippet of code, and then accidentally copying something else right after that. With standard Windows, that copied item is gone forever. It shows your recent clipboard items, lets you search, and lets you pick an item to paste instantly. No switching windows, no disruption to your workflow. It's pure speed.



### 🗂️ Pinning & Favorites
Have an item you use often? Pin it to the top of your clipboard list so it never gets buried under newer copies. Your most important snippets, links, or credentials are always one click away instantly.



### 🔄 Smart Formatting

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

Your clipboard can contain some of your most sensitive data — passwords, financial information, personal messages. Carbon takes this responsibility seriously.

.

**Local-First Architecture:** All your clipboard history, collections, snippets, and OCR extractions are stored locally on your machine. There is no cloud sync, no remote servers, no internet communication whatsoever. Carbon works even when you're completely offline, because everything happens right on your computer.



**No Telemetry:** Carbon doesn't collect usage data, analytics, or any form of telemetry. It doesn't even know that you're using it. You are invisible to us and everyone else except yourself.



**Data Encryption:** Your clipboard history is stored in an encrypted format on your hard drive, so even if someone else accesses your computer, they can't read your clipboard data without your Windows account credentials.



**Full Control:** You decide exactly how long Carbon keeps your history., You can set automatic deletion after a certain period (e.g., 24 hours, 7 days症), clear your entire history manually at any time, or exclude specific apps from being tracked. You are always in control.

## 🎨 Tips & Tricks to Get the Most Out of Carbon

-

**Master the Hotkey:** Learn the overlay hotkey `Ctrl+Shift+V` — it's the fastest way to access your history without touching your mouse. Practice it a few times until it becomes muscle memory.


**Use Collections for Projects:** If you're working on multiple projects, create a collection for each one. As you copy reference materials, links, or code for a project, drag them into that project's collection. At the end of the day, everything you need is in one organized place.**


**Leverage OCR to Digitize Text:** Take screenshots of physical documents, whiteboards, or even handwritten notes, and Carbon's OCR will make that text searchable forever. It's like having a personal scanner that works with your clipboard.




**Organize Snippets with Variables:** If you're a power user, some snippets support placeholder variables. For example, save a snippet like "Dear {{name}}, thank you for your order" — when you insert it, Carbon asks time fill in the variable. It's a tiny automation that saves time repeatedly.


**Set a History Limit:** Prevent your clipboard history from growing indefinitely by setting a maximum number of items to keep. Carbon will automatically delete the oldest items when the limit is reached, keeping everything snappy and manageable.
